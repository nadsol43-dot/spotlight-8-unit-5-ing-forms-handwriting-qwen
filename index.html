The -ing Form of Verbs

—инг форма глагола — Handwritten Notes

🎯 Core Concept

The -ing form (герундий / причастие) is used in 8 main situations in English grammar.

1

As a Subject (Подлежащее)

The -ing form acts as the subject of the sentence.

✅ Swimming is a good form of exercise.
‍️ Subject = Swimming
2

After Specific Verbs

Certain verbs are always followed by the -ing form.

Verbs: admit, appreciate, avoid, consider, continue, deny, fancy, go (activities), imagine, mind, miss, practise, prevent, quit, save, suggest
✅ Would you mind closing the window?
🚪 mind + closing
3

After Preference Verbs

love, like, enjoy, prefer, dislike, hate — for general preferences.

✅ Chris prefers eating home-cooked meals.
🍽️ prefers + eating
BUT! For special / specific preference → use INFINITIVE:
✅ John would prefer to eat at a restaurant tonight.
would prefer + to eat
4

After Fixed Expressions

Set phrases that always take the -ing form.

Expressions: be busy, it's no use, it's no good, it's (not) worth, what's the use of, can't help, there's no point (in), can't stand, have difficulty (in), have trouble
✅ What's the use of buying a car when you don't even have a driver's license?
use of + buying
5

After spend / waste / lose + time

When talking about time spent on an activity.

✅ George spent two hours trying to fix the DVD player.
spent + two hours + trying
6

After Prepositions & Phrases with "to"

Here "to" is a preposition, not part of an infinitive.

Phrases: look forward to, be/get used to, in addition to, object to
✅ The children look forward to getting a dog.
🐕 look forward to + getting
7

After Other Prepositions (as Object)

The -ing form acts as the object of a preposition.

✅ John is an expert at fixing computers.
💻 at + fixing
✅ Sophie insisted on paying for dinner.
💳 on + paying
8

After Perception Verbs

hear, listen to, notice, see, watch, feel

✅ She watched her son playing with his toys.
watched + playing → action IN PROGRESS (наблюдала за действием в процессе)
BUT! For COMPLETED action → use bare infinitive (без частицы to):
✅ She watched her son play with his teddy bear and then put it away.
watched + play → action COMPLETED (действия были завершены)

🧠 Memory Aid: The "ING" Decision Tree

1. Is it the SUBJECT of the sentence?
⬇️
YES → Use -ing ✅ (Swimming is fun)
⬇️ NO
2. Is there a preposition before it?
⬇️
YES → Use -ing ✅ (good at fixing)
⬇️ NO
3. Is it after a specific verb?
⬇️
YES → Check the verb list!
• General preference (love, enjoy) → -ing
• "would prefer" → to + infinitive
• Perception verb + in progress → -ing
• Perception verb + completed → bare infinitive
⬇️ NO
4. Is it a fixed expression?
️
YES → Use -ing ✅
⬇️ NO
Review the rules! 🔄

Key Takeaway

The -ing form is used after prepositions, as a subject, after certain verbs, and to describe actions in progress. Always check for exceptions like "would prefer + to" and perception verbs with completed actions!
